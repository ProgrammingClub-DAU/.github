# Community Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

As members and contributors of **Programming Club, DAU** at **Dhirubhai Ambani University**, we are committed to providing a welcoming, inclusive, and encouraging environment for everyone. We participate in this club to learn, collaborate, and grow together as student developers.

We welcome individuals of all skill levels, backgrounds, identities, and perspectives.

---

## Expected Behavior

We expect all members and contributors to uphold the following standards:

- **Be Respectful & Empathetic**: Treat fellow students, contributors, and maintainers with kindness and respect.
- **Embrace Inclusivity**: Welcome beginners and accommodate different learning speeds. Remember that everyone was once a beginner.
- **Collaborate Openly**: Share knowledge, encourage experimentation, and support peer growth.
- **Provide Constructive Feedback**: Offer code reviews and critique with patience and encouragement. Focus on the code, not the person.
- **Communicate Professionally**: Maintain constructive, civil language in issues, PRs, club chats, and in-person sessions.

---

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behaviors are unacceptable within our community online or in-person:

- Harassment, derogatory comments, or personal attacks.
- Offensive, discriminatory, or exclusionary jokes and language.
- Publishing others' private information (such as personal email or contact numbers) without permission.
- Trolling, spamming, or sustained disruption of club events and discussions.
- Any form of academic dishonesty or plagiarized code submissions represented as original work.

---

## Enforcement & Reporting Guidelines

### Reporting an Issue
If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please report it to the club leadership:

- **Email**: [programming-club@dau.ac.in](mailto:programming-club@dau.ac.in)

All reports will be reviewed promptly and handled with discretion and confidentiality.

### Maintainer Responsibilities
Club maintainers and student officers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior. If unacceptable behavior occurs, maintainers may take appropriate action, including:

1. **Private Warning**: A private conversation clarifying the violation and explaining expected future behavior.
2. **Temporary Pause**: Temporary suspension from club repositories, discussion channels, or events.
3. **Permanent Removal**: Permanent removal from the organization repositories and club activities for severe or repeated violations, in consultation with university faculty advisors if necessary.

---

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all official organization repositories, communication channels, social media pages, and physical events organized by **Programming Club, DAU** at **Dhirubhai Ambani University**.
